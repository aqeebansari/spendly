Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. While using Spendly, we store the expense information you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions. We do not collect payment card details or bank account credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to provide and improve Spendly: to sign you in, to save and display your expenses, and to produce summaries of your spending. We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the application's database and protected with reasonable technical safeguards, including hashed passwords. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. You may delete your expenses at any time, and you can ask us to delete your account and associated data.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when pages load. We do not share your expense data with any third party. If we add other services in future, we will update this policy.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want your data removed, please contact the Spendly team at support@spendly.example.
